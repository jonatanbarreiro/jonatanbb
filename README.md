# jonatanbb.xyz

My personal site: an index of the things I make, each a node wired to the bb signet.
It began as a CV page built from scratch in the open; that site, its making-of and its
whole chain of iterations now live in [`cvsite/`](cvsite/), and it is served at
jonatanbb.xyz/cv/.


## The index

The signet sits on a triangular lattice, wired to six nodes on a hexagon. Code walks
the lattice in its six directions, and what half its heads run into lights up: a link
or a node carries the hit out to its arrow and in to the capacitor at the signet's
heart, whose charge is the signet's only light and whose kick sets its rings turning,
and the words the lattice hides surface where they are struck. Where two heads collide
a pop-up is decoded out of the code: a quote, or an equation of my own, cut from the
scans of my handwritten notes.

The site is half of a duality, designed as one with [firecoat.me](https://firecoat.me):
here form and numbers, cold metal and light; there meaning and words, stone and fire.
So the palette is ink `#1c1a16`, white, and gold as the colour of light, with the
firecoat node, in carmesí, the one warm thing let in.

Open `index/index.html` straight from disk to browse it: the nodes then lead to the
local copies of their sites. [terminology.md](terminology.md) names its parts.


## The repo

`index/` is the source; `gallery/` shows how the site looked after each iteration,
`prompts/` keeps every session's log verbatim, and [milestones.md](milestones.md)
tracks where jonatanbb.xyz is headed, version by version.
