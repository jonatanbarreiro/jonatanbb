# jonatanbb.xyz —design brief

What has been written about how this site should look and feel, gathered from every
project in `~/Escritorio` up to 16 November 2026 and weighed towards what repeats and
what is newest. It has a twin, `../firecoat/design-brief.md`: the two sites are designed
as one duality, and each brief is half of a picture. Three sketches built from it sit
beside this file —see *The sketches*.


## What the site is

The public side of Jonatan: an index of the things he makes, drawn as a small network.
The centre node introduces him and the site in a few sentences and links the CV site and
the projects; each project node opens into a preview of its project. The site is the
dressing, not the product —its job is to make the projects easy to reach and good to
look at, and then get out of the way.

It goes live when there is something to show: the aim is 1g49's proof of concept around
12 December 2026, with the 3D site for CB Teo as the flagship fallback, and penquire and
firecoat as the other nodes. The longer arc, in his words: a CV page, then a portfolio,
then a professional profile, and eventually "my interface to the internet".


## The duality

Every design decision can be checked against this table. jonatanbb.xyz is always the
left column.

| jonatanbb.xyz | firecoat.me |
|---|---|
| form, syntactic | meaning, semantic |
| numbers —mathematics | words —writing |
| cold, metallic, light, austere | warm, stone and wood, dark, fire, giants |
| processed, correct, simple, results | raw, true, complex, effort |
| restrained, the face one presents | unapologetic, honest |
| data-driven | story-driven |
| up there —the solar system, the galaxy | down here —on the ground, under the night sky |
| the armillary sphere: hollow, calculated, aseptic | the celestial sphere: named constellations, full of meaning |
| a metal Newton on a fountain | a nondescript stone giant by a bonfire |
| Russell: beauty "cold and austere, like that of sculpture" | Newton: "on the shoulders of giants" |
| the b sharp, many-faced, concave | the b smooth, flame-like |
| the bb form, gold | the db form, carmesí |
| code falling, equations as its fixed points | words rising, weaving what is read |
| left | right |

Newton is shown here and meant there. That asymmetry is deliberate: it is Jonatan
placing himself towards firecoat.


## The image

Empty space, and in it an armillary sphere: the index page itself. Its rings are the
annulus of the bb signet, dislodged —the one ring of the logo come apart into several
and tilted off its plane. At its centre the sun and the two b's shine through. Light
beams leave it towards the pages it indexes; they are the links. On the horizon,
atmospheric light draws equations. Nothing props the sphere up from a base: a metal
Newton holds it, standing on a fountain.

Around that image, the motifs he keeps returning to:

- **The network.** The site began as one node (the CV site) and is now a signet wired to
  six on a hexagon. "Node" is a way of picturing a concept, not a technical term. Point
  clouds, graphs and diagrams are where the idea comes from, and the CV site already
  winks at them: blue-noise points, a pulse that reveals the graph between them.
- **Hexagons and the Fibonacci sphere.** Hexagonal movement over Cartesian up-and-down;
  the Fibonacci sphere as the way to spread points over a sphere —it is also what his
  LitS research samples directions with.
- **The Matrix.** Code in the background, with equations arising as its visible fixed
  points. The known difficulty: digital rain falls one way, which sits badly in a 3D or
  2.5D scene.
- **Field lines.** Light paths that follow literal electromagnetic paths rather than
  straight segments —a thought left unfinished.
- **Equations from penquire.** The handwriting of his own notebooks, cleaned to ink
  alone, is the source of every equation on the site.


## Materials

**Palette.** Ink `#1c1a16`, a warm near-black; white; and gold as the colour of light
—`#fad02a` is the CV site's official gold, `#ffe666` its glow, `#e8a317` the signet's
gold as firecoat keeps it. The metal is satin black that turns gold where light reaches
it, which is how the CV site already lights the logo. One warm colour is allowed in: the
firecoat node, in carmesí `#7f3445`.

**Type.** P052 —the Palatino lineage: URW Palladio, TeX Gyre Pagella— for everything
read; small caps for the eyebrow, as on the CV site; a monospace only for code and data.

**The signet.** Two b's in a ring, exact geometry in `index/assets/bb-mark.svg`. The b is
one stroked centreline whose outline is a 12-corner polygon
(`cvsite/assets/illumination/bb.txt`), which is what a faceted 3D b is cut from.

**Light.** Light is drawn with gold and with what it does to ink, never with glare: on a
white page, glow is a soft gold stain and a lit edge.


## Structure

The centre node: name, two or three sentences, and the links out. The nodes as of this
brief: the CV site (`/cv/`), 1g49, the CB Teo club site, penquire, firecoat.me, and
LitS as the research node. A node that is not ready shows its preview and no link.
Each node carries data rather than a pitch —a version, a count, a date.

The page text is Jonatan's. Every word in the sketches is a stand-in lifted from his own
copy where it existed.

## Open questions

- **Light or dark.** "Light" and "austere" pull towards a bright void; "empty space",
  the solar system and the galaxy pull towards a dark one. Sketches 1 and 2 are bright,
  sketch 3 is dark.
- **Which gold.** `#fad02a` or `#e8a317`. The sketches use the first for light and the
  second for metal.
- **How much Newton.** A likeness, an attribute, or only a pose. The sketches give him
  the pose of Sargent's Atlas, mirrored, so the two sites hold their spheres the same
  way.
- **The fountain's water.** The sketches make it the digital rain: digits fall as water
  does, which gives the rain a place where falling one way is natural.
- **Left and right.** Read here as layout —this site's text and weight sit left,
  firecoat's right. It may have been meant less literally.
- **The nodes.** Whether LitS and the giants site belong, and whether CB Teo is a node
  of its own or hangs from 1g49.
- **A night mode.** The CV site has one. The index sketches do not, so that the dark
  stays firecoat's.


## The sketches

Three takes on the same brief, each paired with a firecoat sketch of the same number.
None is functional beyond what its look needs. Open each `index.html` straight from
disk.

- `sketch1_armillary/` —the image as written: the signet's ring dislodges into the five
  bands of an armillary sphere, held by a faceted metal Newton on a fountain whose water
  is digits, in a bright void. Each node opens as a lens where its beam lands, and one
  formula at a time is written on the horizon. Twin: firecoat's `sketch1_bonfire/`.
- `sketch2_lattice/` —the placeholder grown up: ink on white, a triangular lattice that
  code walks in six directions, and handwritten equations in the corners where it
  settles. Every node is a lens that already shows its project. Twin:
  `sketch2_wordrain/`.
- `sketch3_orrery/` —up there, and the dark take: the signet as a sun, the nodes on its
  orbits. Two of the unfinished thoughts get an answer to judge: the sky is a Fibonacci
  sphere of code whose streams run along its spiral arms instead of falling one way, and
  each link is the field line of a dipole through its node. Equations are written along
  a horizon of atmospheric light. Twin: `sketch3_gemberland/`.

Each folder stands on its own, and the same few files recur in them: `bb.js`, the logo
as geometry, flat and in three dimensions; `nodes.js`, the nodes as data with the
miniature each opens into; `eq.js`, ten formulas cut from penquire's scans down to their
ink; and `figure.js`, the kneeling figure, which firecoat's giant is cast from too.
