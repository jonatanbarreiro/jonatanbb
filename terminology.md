# Terminology

The words coined for the parts of jonatanbb.xyz. A word used in its common sense
stays out.

- bb form —the two b's as this site sets them: side by side, both facing right, the
  left one lower; gold, where the db form is carmesí.
- capacitor —the hexagon at the signet's centre, between the two b's, and its only
  light: a pulse charges it, and it drains steadily back to black.
- db form —the two b's as firecoat.me sets them: the left one mirrored into a d, the
  two facing each other.
- head —the glyph at the front of a stream, which walks the lattice point by point
  and strikes whatever it runs into; one in two passes through the signet, links and
  nodes instead.
- link —the line from its terminal, a hexagon like the capacitor on the coarse hexagon
  about the signet's centre and glowing as the capacitor does, out through a node,
  which a head striking it sets pulsing both ways; it runs on to where its node's far
  vertex lies when open, and ends there in the db form's arrow.
- node —one of the hexagons wired to the signet, each standing for a site or project
  and linking out to it: closed, it holds a small icon; open, four levels larger, a
  big one.
- pop-up —what two heads raise into the lattice where they collide, decoded out of the
  code: an equation from the notes, or a quote.
- pulse —the light a strike sends along a link: inwards it turns the signet's bands,
  outwards it heats the node and may leave the arrow as a new head.
- signet —the bb form in its ring, the site's mark. At the centre of the index the
  ring is cut into three bands, one for each axis of the lattice, which pulses set
  turning.
- stream —a head and the trail of glyphs it leaves behind it, fading as it goes.
