# Terminology

The words coined for the parts of jonatanbb.xyz. A word used in its common sense
stays out.

- bb form —the two b's as this site sets them: side by side, both facing right, the
  left one lower; gold, where the db form is carmesí.
- db form —the two b's as firecoat.me sets them: the left one mirrored into a d, the
  two facing each other.
- head —the glyph at the front of a stream, which walks the lattice point by point
  and strikes whatever it runs into.
- node —one of the hexagons wired to the signet, each standing for a site or project
  and linking out to it: closed, it holds a small icon; open, two levels larger, a
  big one.
- signet —the bb form in its ring, the site's mark. At the centre of the index the
  ring is cut into three bands, one for each axis of the lattice, which pulses set
  turning.
- stream —a head and the trail of glyphs it leaves behind it, fading as it goes.
